Aurora Borealis

→ Classic aurora → Magnetosphere variant

An interactive generative artwork exploring auroral dynamics through layered noise fields, particle motion, and physically-inspired flow. The system mimics the behaviour of charged particles guided by Earth’s magnetic field, blending scientific intuition with aesthetic design.

The project now exists in two forms: a classic aurora renderer and an experimental magnetosphere variant introducing vortex-driven flow, local disturbances, and solar-wind-like interactions.

Status: active development

Next steps:

  • Refine magnetic flow coherence and vortex structure
  • Improve palette separation and atmospheric depth
  • Introduce interactive space-weather parameters (e.g. activity level)
  • Explore data-driven modulation and AI-assisted styling
